The two ReMind files are checked against the release's SHA256SUMS. The UMT5 encoder and the Wan2.2 VAE are read from their torch.save archives remotely, tensor by tensor, without unpickling code. None of the 20 GB Wan2.2 DiT shards is needed: the ReMind generator supplies every tensor.
